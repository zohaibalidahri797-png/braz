What the limits mean

Bots — concurrent bots on your account. Storage — per-bot workspace size (project + dependencies). Deployments — builds started per day. Log retention — how many log lines you can tail. Runtime policy per bot: memory/CPU/disk limits enforced by the execution runtime; bots that abuse resources are stopped, not billed.
